# Pet Rock 🪨

> **The only pet that lives forever on the blockchain.**

In a world of complex DeFi protocols, volatile tokens, and endless noise, **Pet Rock** offers something different: **Stability. Simplicity. Serenity.**

It is the ultimate digital companion for the Farcaster era. It doesn't ask for food. It doesn't need walks. It just sits there, being a rock, waiting for your affection.

---

## 💎 The Philosophy

**Pet Rock** is a return to basics. It is a meditation on digital ownership and interaction.

*   **Zero Maintenance**: Your rock is self-sufficient. It thrives on neglect but blossoms with attention.
*   **Infinite Patience**: It will wait for you. Forever.
*   **Unbreakable Bond**: Your interactions are personal. The rock remembers every pet, every moment you spend together.

## 🎮 How to Interact

### 1. The Petting Ritual
Simply tap the hand icon to pet your rock. Watch as it responds with a subtle, satisfying animation. It's tactile, responsive, and incredibly grounding.

### 2. Build Your Streak
Your total pet count is a badge of honor. It represents your dedication to the simple things in life.

### 3. Share the Zen
Integrated directly with Farcaster, you can share your petting milestones with your network. Let your friends know that you—and your rock—are doing just fine.

---

## 📱 Farcaster & Base Mini App

Pet Rock is a fully integrated **Mini App**, designed to run natively within:

*   **Farcaster Clients**: Engage with your rock directly in **Warpcast** frames.
*   **Base App**: Experience the app with full wallet integration on the **Base** network.

It demonstrates the power of the **Farcaster SDK (MiniKit)** and **OnchainKit** to create seamless social-crypto experiences.
