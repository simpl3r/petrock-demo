# Pet Rock 🪨

> **The ultimate digital companion for the Farcaster ecosystem.**

**Pet Rock** is a native **Mini App** designed to live harmoniously within **Base** and **Farcaster** (Warpcast). It brings the timeless charm of a pet rock into the onchain era, providing a persistent, interactive experience that integrates deeply with your digital identity.

---

## 🌟 Why Pet Rock?

Pet Rock is more than just a clicker—it's a persistent digital entity that travels with you.

### 🔗 Deep Ecosystem Integration
Built exclusively for the **Base** and **Farcaster** ecosystem, Pet Rock leverages the **OnchainKit** to provide a seamless native experience. It feels less like a web page and more like a core part of your social client.

### 👤 Personalized Experience
Your rock knows you. By integrating directly with Farcaster Context, the app recognizes your **Identity**, greeting you by name and adapting to your profile. It's not just *a* rock; it's *your* rock.

### 📢 Native Social Sharing
Proud of your petting streaks? Share your milestones directly to your Farcaster feed with a single tap. Our integrated "Share" functionality composes casts that look native and engaging, driving social interaction without friction.

### 💾 Persistent Bonding
Your relationship with your rock is saved locally and persists across sessions. Come back days later, and your progress remains.

---

## ⚙️ Configuration & Customization

Pet Rock is designed to be flexible. Administrators can control the experience using simple Environment Variables:

| Variable | Function |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`NEXT_PUBLIC_PROJECT_NAME`** | The public facing name of your app instance. |
| **`NEXT_PUBLIC_SHOW_GREETING`** | `true` / `false` — Toggle the personalized welcome message ("Hi, @farcaster"). |
| **`NEXT_PUBLIC_PETROCK_COUNT_VERSION`** | A version string (e.g., `v1`). Updating this value acts as a "Season Reset," giving all users a fresh start. |
